# Forest Run — All-family ordinary random admission lead proof (2026-09-29)

## Why this check exists

Ordinary random sequencing now waits for the previous persistence-owning encounter to resolve and for the real Player to return to RUNNING/LANDING. Production also stages a new encounter with `SpawnPacing.minimumRandomEncounterLeadPx`, intended to preserve a conservative sampled full-jump response window even after the new entity consumes its creation frame. Prior integration evidence exercised the generic selected family and the fastest immediate Hedgehog, but did not enumerate every ordinary family or surface class. A family whose collision geometry begins left of its nominal origin, or a staged entity that retires before entry, could therefore violate the intended cross-family lead while the generic test remained green.

## Regression

The Robolectric integration derives the union of all five biome ordinary pools and requires it to equal all 19 authored `EntityType` values. Across 360, 720, 1080 and 1440-pixel landscape heights it filters only production-ineligible Bamboo, stages every remaining family at the exact production action-safe origin (forcing hazard Dog), advances the real Player then EntityManager in production order for one maximum admitted frame at the speed ceiling, and requires:
- the entity remains staged;
- no creation-frame collision resolves;
- the live encounter leading edge remains finite and ahead of the Player;
- the remaining lead still satisfies the same sampled 495px full-jump decision+safety envelope under the conservative 1.15x immediate-approach ceiling;
- the simpler duck decision budget also remains available.

This is source-level timing/geometry evidence. It does not claim that every family has the same correct response, that authored debug overlap scenarios are fair, or that a human can perceive every telegraph on real hardware. Compact Bamboo remains deliberately excluded where its complete five-stalk authored tunnel cannot physically fit.
